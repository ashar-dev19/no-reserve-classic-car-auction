The Two Rules

How bidding resolves

A bidder names a ceiling, not a price

The system bids for them, one step at a time, and only as high as the competition forces. A bidder willing to pay $40,000 beats a $25,000 rival at $25,250 and keeps the rest. The ceiling is never shown to anyone.

The last second cannot steal the lot

Any bid in the final two minutes pushes the close back out by two minutes, without limit. A lot ends when bidding stops, not when the clock runs out — so nobody has to sit at a screen to defend a position.
